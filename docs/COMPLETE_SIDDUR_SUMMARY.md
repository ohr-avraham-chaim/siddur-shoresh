## Thematic Categories

### Creation & Formation

**Two Creation Verbs:**
- **ב-ר-א** (bara - create ex nihilo) → 2x (Asher Yatzar, Elohai Neshama)
- **י-צ-ר** (yatzar - form/shape) → 3x (Asher Yatzar, Elohai Neshama)

**Usage**:
- Asher Yatzar: "**יָצַר** אֶת הָאָדָם... וּ**בָרָא** בו נְקָבִים"
- First formed (general), then created (specific internal systems)

**ק-ד-ש** (sanctify, holy) → appears in:

- Theme: Transmission of wisdom across generations

## Linguistic Insights

### Word Patterns

**Repetition for Emphasis** (appears in 2 texts):

1. **Asher Yatzar**: "נְקָבִים נְקָבִים חֲלוּלִים חֲלוּלִים"
   - "orifices orifices, hollow-spaces hollow-spaces"
   - Emphasizes multiplicity and complexity

2. **Eilu Devarim 1**: "שֶׁאֵין לָהֶם שִׁעוּר"
   - "that have no measure/limit"
   - Lists mitzvot without quantitative limits

### Prefixes & Suffixes

**Common Prefixes**:
- **ב** (in, with): בְּמִצְותָיו (with His commandments)
- **ו** (and): וְצִוָּנוּ (and commanded us)
- **ה** (the): הָעולָם (the world)
- **ל** (to, for): לַעֲסוק (to engage)

**Common Suffixes**:
- **נוּ** (us/our): אֱלהֵינוּ (our God), קִדְּשָׁנוּ (sanctified us)
- **ךָ** (your/you): תורָתְךָ (Your Torah), שְׁמֶךָ (Your Name)
- **ים** (plural): נְקָבִים (orifices), הָעַמִּים (the nations)

### Roots with Single Letters

Some roots are single letters (grammatical particles):
- **ל** (to, for) → 13x
- **ו** (and) → embedded in countless words
- **ב** (in, with) → embedded in countless words
